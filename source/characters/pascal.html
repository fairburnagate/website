About

Note: This is a Limbus Company character based off of Pascal from NieR:Automata.

Pascal is the designated 16th sinner of Limbus Company’s LCB department. She is a bashful person with a tendency to hide herself from others, barely speaking to anyone aside from a few choice words.

She previously worked for an orphanage in the District 9 backstreets, and has also spent a number of years working as a fixer prior to joining Limbus Company.

Appearance

Pascal is a tall, dark-skinned woman of average build, with dark green eyes and light brown hair. Her uniform is comprised with a long black that has a hood (for the purpose of hiding her face), a white dress shirt, black gloves, black pants, and combat boots. She also wears several bows in her head in different bright colors, which contrasts her dark outfit.

In combat, she wears a large mechanical arm on a harness that has a circular saw attached to it. The saw blade has “PEUR” written along it.

Story

Pre/During Smoke War

Pascal was born in the District 9 backstreets with her mother as her sole surviving family member. She began Fixer work at a young age to be able to provide for her household, subsequently participating in the Smoke War for a chance to be able to migrate her and her mother to a nest. Witnessing the horrors of the Smoke War with her own eyes, she deserted the war and swore to herself that she would not continue this cycle of violence.

Post Smoke War

Following the Smoke War, she began working at an orphanage in District 9’s backstreets, taking care of the children and teaching them. She made sure to teach them everything she knew—how to care for themselves, how to defend themselves, and, most importantly, how to survive. During her time working at the orphanage, the Pianist distortion manifested in District 9 and began causing havoc. The other workers of the orphanage were killed, forcing Pascal to evacuate the children to a shelter by herself. She then left temporarily to find other survivors. Upon her return to the shelter, she discovered that all the children had committed suicide out of fear, swords having pierced their hearts.

Left to stare at the aftermath of what she had done, she attempted to commit suicide and ultimately failed and broke down. She was recruited by Limbus Company during this time with the promise to make her forget.
