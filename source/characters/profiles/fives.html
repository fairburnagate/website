Fives is a… man? Of unknown origin. He is one of the sole residents of The Sub-Zero. (or lives in project dignity HQ?)

He currently works for Project Dignity as the pilot for the Extraction Department, extracting the bodies of the victims of The Salvation Massacre and returning them to the quarters. Furthermore, he is currently the only known person who’s able to venture out into Ja-han.

He is also an impromptu bodyguard for John Doe.

Appearance

He has buzzed-down hair, brown skin, and green-grey eyes. The right half of his face appears to be melting into itself. This could also be said for his right arm and a small bit of his chest.

There is a hole where his left eye should be, going all the way into the back of his head. Some holes can be seen on the left side of his neck. The right side of his neck has abnormally large needle marks on it.

He wears a black hoodie, black pants, black boots, and aircrew gloves.
While working with John Doe, he is seen wearing a white button-up with a black tie, black pants, black boots, and aircrew gloves.

When he goes outside, he puts wraps bandages on his neck and the left side of his face.

Story

Born in the lower-layer of Iemel, Fives (Actual name: Nafas Alhaya) was neglected for as long as he can remember by his parents in favor of *keeping the smoke away*. Being the only one able to provide for his household—as his parents were far too weak to do anything due to years upon years of smoke inhalation—he set out to become a pilot to get a steady pay and to get his parents out of that town.

As a teenager, he worked multiple jobs and did a lot of "favors" in order to be able to pay for him and Miller to travel to the upper-layer of Iemel.

In college, he met Tom Ien, and they quickly began a relationship. He went missing months later, and Tom just so happened to move away around that time.

hree years later, during May, he woke up in an undisclosed location—the only thing he remembered being that look on Tom's face. This location had turned out to be the Project Dignity Headquarters, where he was subsequently turned into their lab rat (much to his displeasure,) and recruited into their Extraction department as an "explorative pilot."

He took in Carrion after finding him in his apartment, and decided to give up his care to Lucie after about 9 months due to feeling like he is unable to take care of him.

After first meeting John Doe, he became their unplanned bodyguard and now lives with them in Nisf for reasons beyond his comprehension. On especially long missions, he lives in the Project Dignity Headquarters with Jean.
