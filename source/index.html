About Me

Hello. I'm Saif. I use he/him/xe/xem/xeir pronouns. I made this tacky website so I don't have to put my things on 50 different websites. I'm still new to HTML, so the website might look a little rough.

I am a guy-adjacent being who likes coffee, tea, drawing, and rocks. My favourite rock is Botswana Agate, and my favourite type of tea is Earl Grey.

My favourite characters are Pascal (NieR:Automata) and Kim Kitsuragi (Disco Elysium).

I speak both (Egyptian) Arabic and English. I have OCD and autism, both of which affect the way I act.

Feel free to send me a message anytime, I'll respond as quickly as I can. Please note that my way of talking may sound dry.

Please send me an e-mail if you experience any issues with the website.
Unfortunately, the website doesn't look all that great on mobile, but it's still usable.

If you haven't visited in a while, please hard refresh using Ctrl+Shift+R, or long press the refresh button on mobile.

Uses

This is a list of all of the stuff I use! I saw a bunch of these on uses.tech, so I decided to make my own because I have a lot of software I use.

I don't know why I have so much software that does the same thing. I should probably do something about this.

Hardware

ThinkPad X395

I got this beautiful thing second-hand and it's never failed me once. Though, the SD Card tray is missing...

  • SSD: 500 GB
  • RAM: 16 GB
  • Operating System: Linux Mint 22.3

Samsung Galaxy A36

When I first got this phone, one of the apps (called AppCloud I think?) auto-downloaded a bunch of mobile games on there. There's no way to have it *not* do that, and you have to remove the app with ADB. So weird.

  • Storage: 256GB
  • RAM: 8GB
  • Operating System: Android 16

Lenovo Vibe B

I recently repurposed this little thing into an MP3 player! I had a hard time trying to install a certificate so I could get F-Droid running, but everything worked out in the end.

It will spend the rest of its life running VLC Media Player. Isn't that beautiful?

  • Storage: 8GB (+64GB microSD)
  • Operating System: Android 6

Samsung Galaxy Tab S10 FE

Sadly, my iPad Pro has seen the end of its life, and thankfully I don't need to use lightning cables anymore! It feels so refreshing to use something that can hold a charge for more than 30 minutes, and also be able to use Kirta for Android (and just Android in general.) I didn't have any issues with AppCloud on this device.

The tablet also came with a pen inside the box, unlike with Apple's devices. I find the rubber tip that comes with it a bit annoying when I'm drawing and/or writing, though…

  • Storage: 256GB
  • RAM: 12GB
  • Operating System: Android 16

soundcore H30i

Pretty great headphones! There's not much to say about these, but do note that the cushions start to peel after a while. They also tend to flop around a lot.

QCY ArcBuds Lite

I'm actually not really into Active Noise Cancellation, so it's nice to have something that's cheap and also lets me be aware of my surroundings. The battery life is also pretty good (I'm looking at you, AirPods.)

CASIO WSC1250H-1AV

I really enjoy looking at the little fish at the top (even though I don't even fish), and the moon phase is also great. I've noticed that ever since I got this watch, I feel uneasy whenever I don't have something cutting the blood flow to my hand while I'm outside.

Also, at the time of writing this I've found out that this watch has been discontinued, which is devastating to say the least.

Software

Linux

i3 - Window Manager

I haven't really been able to use Desktops Environments properly ever since i started using i3. It's really convenient to have everything tiled up, and to be able to navigate things using the keyboard.

i3blocks - i3 Feed Generator

Love being able to see my RAM usage right at the top. The mediaplayer thing broke for me a while ago after working perfectly fine beforehand, though.

picom - Compositor

I got it since i3 doesn't have its own compositor. Adds some nice animations.

KeePassXC - Password Manager

I switched from Bitwarden to KeePassXC mostly out of boredom but also because I don't want to rely on the cloud too much. I've been using it with Syncthing and I enjoy the layout of it much more.

LibreWolf - Browser

A friend of mine told me about this fork of Firefox. It's amazing being able to browse without any of the AI slop features that Mozilla forces on you. Do note that you have to turn features like Sync on manually.

Extensions: uBlock Origin, Violentmonkey, KeePassXC-Browser, SponsorBlock, Indie Wiki Buddy

Strawberry - Music

I can listen to my favourite online radio stations here and also have all of my music be put into a huge, shuffled playlist like with foobar2000 on windows! There's also a Nyan Cat analyzer.

MusicBrainz Picard - Music Tagger

This has been a lifesaver for tagging and renaming my songs. If you're still renaming your stuff manually PLEASE have a look.

Obsidian - Notes

I definitely haven't yet discovered just how many things Obsidian can do. I want to try to make a media database with it soon.

Krita - Drawing

I've been using Krita for years and it's never failed me. The default brushes are so good that I never downloaded external ones. If you want an amazing free drawing program please check it out!!

PureRef - Reference Image Viewer

I use this alongside Krita. One of the things I like is that you're able to save the little image boards you make and open them later.

RSS Guard - RSS Reader

I find the layout of this reader much more pleasant than Akregators, though I can't find ANY way to make the font bigger for some reason.

Vesktop - Discord Client

A certain individual forces me to call him on Discord a few times a week, and I enjoy that I don't have to install Discord and Vencord seperately.

Lite XL / VSCodium - Code Editors

I've had this issue for a while where Lite XL automatically closes after a certain amount of time, and it also doesn't fare well with i3. Very sad because I wanted a less RAM-hungry code editor and you couldn't pay me to use Neovim.

Syncthing - File Sync

The best sync solution I could ever ask for. I have my Obsidian vault and music synced to my phone, and also my website in case something happens to my laptop.

kitty - Terminal Emulator

What can I say? it's a very nice and fast terminal emulator and I enjoy the amount of themes included with it. I currently use the "Argonaut" theme.

JDownloader - Download Manager

I usually only use this to download comics or to bypass those annoying piracy websites that use hosts like 1fichier. I don't like that you have to debloat it, though...

Nicotine+ - Soulseek Client

*So* much better for downloading albums than any website could ever be, but there's a small chance you'll get harassed by a French person at any given time.

iloader - iOS Sideloading

I've been trying day and night to set up SideStore with this thing, and when I finally manage to do it, SideStore tells me "invalid format" so I still have to sideload with my laptop. Ugh!

Calibre - E-book Manager / Reader

Like with Obsidian, I haven't even scratched the surface of what I can do with Calibre, but I *LOVE* the metadata and conversion tools.

MComix - Comic Reader

Perfect for my unique way of reading comics, and the image enhancement feature is great.

ComicTagger

Does exactly what's on the box, but I've noticed it's not very nice with AMM.

VirtualBox - Virtual Machines

Every once in a while I have to use Windows for something, and the unattended setup helps me not pull my hair out during the account setup. I also like trying out different Linux Distributions on there.

xpad - Sticky Notes

Having a sticky note full of HTML snippets for my website is extremely helpful! I recommend you try it out. xpad is also nice to i3 so it works out.

Konversation - IRC Client

I use this exclusively for pirating e-books. Maybe one day I'll start actually talking to people on there...

qBittorrent - Torrent Client

Because I'm in a country that doesn't care (that much) about piracy, I prefer torrenting much more than direct downloads because of how much faster it is most of the time.

Flameshot - Screenshot Tool

It's pretty good, though I'd much rather be using ShareX.

Gear Lever - AppImage Manager

AppImageLauncher broke for me recently, so I had to move to this application only for it to break RSS Guard with an update. It's nice to me 80% of the time, though.

Android

Kvaesitso - App Launcher

I always search for my apps, so why not just get a search-based app launcher? I find it much more pleasant to use than One UI, even with the Good Lock customisations.

F-Droid - App Store

About half of my apps come from F-Droid. I hope to one day be free from the shackles of the Google Play Store.

Obtainium - APK Installer / Updater

I use Obtainium for when there's no F-Droid download available.

Wikipedia

Who doesn't want Wikipedia on their phone? There's an iOS version, too.

Fennec - Browser

Firefox with telemetry removed. What more can I say?

Extensions: uBlock Origin, Violentmonkey

FUTO - Keyboard

Couldn't ask for a better keyboard. I find the swipe typing experience much better than SwiftKey.

Fossify Gallery

I really love the fact you can hide certain folders and that you can remove EXIF data from photos.

KeePassDX - Password Manager

It has much better autofill than Bitwarden in my opinion. I use this alongside BasicSync.

ArchiveTune / Foobar2000 - Music

I use ArchiveTune for online music, and Foobar2000 for music I have downloaded locally. Foobar2000 works fine, but I wish it was easier to install themes.

Pano Scrobbler - Music Tracker

Amazing for apps that don't support ListenBrainz, and I use it alongside KDE Connect to track listens on my Lenovo Vibe B since it's unsupported.

Obsidian - Notes

I only ever use Obsidian on my phone for writing posts on the fly, but it's a pretty good app. I use it alongside BasicSync.

Librera - E-book Reader

If for some unknown reason I want to read books on my phone, I have this app ready.

CDisplayEx - Comic Reader

Same with Librera, but with comics. Why do I have so many apps just laying around?

Ente Auth - Authenticator

Two-factor authentication has been nothing but a pain in my ass, but at least I can sleep well at night knowing somebody is less likely to get into my [social media here] account.

Krita for Android - Drawing Program

I'm so glad to finally be able to use Krita on mobile. Please use Krita

KDE Connect - Multi-device Sync

The clipboard sync is more useful than I ever could've imagined. We love KDE in this house.

BasicSync - File Sync

It's a syncthing client. I moved to it from Syncthing-Fork after the whole ownership debacle.

Termux - Terminal Emulator

I use Neovim on here whenever I need to urgently update my website. Otherwise I never touch it for the sake of my sanity (and the fact that I hate using a terminal on a phone).

Morphe - APK Patcher

YouTube is genuinely unusable for me without the Morphe patches. The same goes for Twitter, and Instagram, and basically everything else...

Seal - yt-dlp Wrapper

I download videos with it. There's nothing much to say.

Shizuku Fork - API Access

I use it with Essentials for the app lock because I'm just a tiny bit paranoid.

Canta - Android Debloater

I wish I found out about this and Shizuku before I went through the hassle of downloading ADB to uninstall that AppCloud thing...
