Windows 11 Out of the Box #

Posted December 5th, 2025

I'm among one of the many people who moved to Linux because, well, Windows 11 just sucks. The forced updates, the entire Recall thing, Copilot being forced onto you every single second of your life, and many other things.

I must say that my Windows 11 experience wasn't *that* awful. Why? Because I had to cake on 50 system tweaks just so my system could be usable, and I don't think I have to say that you shouldn't have to do that.

I really enjoy playing around with virtual machines, so I figured, why not try a fresh install of Windows 11? It'd be interesting to see just how different it is from my previous setup.

I'm not going to comment on the speed, since I allocated only 5 GB of RAM to my VM, which Windows eats for breakfast, but I can safely say everything else was awful.

First things first, it tried to force me to connect a Microsoft account, and when I managed to bypass that, I was IMMEDIATELY bombarded with ads on the Start menu. Who the fuck puts ads in a paid operating software? And the taskbar has these "widgets" that I do not care about in any way, and, of course, Copilot is shoved in my face.

I geniunely can't recommend Chris Titus' Winutil enough. I would've gone crazy trying to disable all of the junk on the taskbar without this utility. Though, I must tell you to please not run any command you see on the internet if you don't know what it does.

Something else I noticed is that Notepad has Copilot, and it's locked behind a subscription? I don't understand who exactly would need AI inside of Notepad, and who would need it so badly that they'd pay for it. It also requires you to sign into a Microsoft account, which is nice.

That aside, it does what you expect an operating software to do. That's until the newest update breaks something you're using, like Localhost, or Windows Recovery.

By the way, and this is just my opinion, I think File Explorer sucks. Check out something like Q-Dir or Directory Opus obtained through (cough) legal methods if you'd like an alternative.

I'm not going to be insufferable and call you a moron for using Windows and that you must switch to Linux immediately, but if you've never used Linux before, please give it a chance. Try out a distribution on something like VirtualBox (make sure to have virtualization enabled virst) and see if you like it!

If you don't know which of the many distributions to choose, check out Linux Mint or Pop!_OS. (Please don't let anybody convince you to use Arch as your first distro.) Even better, go on Distro Chooser and see which of the results interests you the most.

Misogyny in Metal Gear Solid #

Posted November 14th, 2025

Let me preface: I love Metal Gear Solid. I feel like I have to say this given how Metal Gear Solid fans are.

When I got into Metal Gear Solid, I saw a very glaring issue, which was the misogyny that hits you in the face in every game. Whether it be every female character being sexualized to hell and back (like with Quiet) or being sidelined or just straight up killed (EVA, Naomi, Strangelove, etc.)

It started with MGS1, with the camera zooming onto Meryl's ass, and Solid Snake harassing her, and whatever else that I blocked out of my memory, and then MGS2, with the "easter eggs" featuring Japanese adult actresses, which made me question if it was worth it to continue onto the next games, and I think I reached my breaking point when I saw Quiet.

She has to breathe through her skin, which forces her to wear basically nothing.. and she doesn't talk (Yes, I know why she doesn't talk, but still.) And there have been more scenes where they zoom onto her chest than I could count.

You could say the constant sexualisation was just MGS being a product of its time, but it still makes my skin crawl. It also doesn't help that (most of) the people in the fanbase will chalk up what you say to pure stupidity or try to invalidate your take by mentioning The Boss, and while I admit that she's a solid character, but that doesn't take away from the misogynistic writing in the games.

I've seen people be called slurs, be sent death threats, and get the most vicious hate I've witnessed at the hands of MGS fans just for having the same takes as I. I don't think I should be told "This is why females should've never joined the gaming community" just for saying a franchise has misogynistic writing.

These are only a few examples. I haven't touched MGS in months so I don't remember much else. If anybody reading this has some thoughts on this, feel free to reach out.

English #

Posted October 14th, 2025

I'm sure you know that English is not my first language, Arabic is, and that's why my english is not that good. You'd expect that I would be better at speaking Arabic, but no. My Arabic is *much* poorer than my English.

I had to teach myself English because, well, how would I hope to make friends on the internet if I don't know how to speak English? One of the consequences is that I get worse and worse at speaking Arabic every day. Hell, even my friends think I'm a foreigner.

It makes me a little sad. It's as if I've been disconnected from who I am, and where I come from. I could tell you right now that I speak Arabic, and I'd be right, but sometimes it feels like I'm lying. That it's not *actually* my mother tongue.

I learned English through watching YouTube videos. I know that it's great that I learned an entire language by myself, but I still feel like I regret it. I feel like I'm being stripped of something inherent to me, day by day. Maybe one day it'll be completely gone? I hope not.

Though, if I never learned English, what friends would I have?

Homosexuality, Religion, and Egypt #

Posted October 11th, 2025

Since the first grade, I've known that I'm in some way queer. I thought that it was quite the normal thing, that how could anybody possibly be upset at you for realising who you truly are?

Obviously, I was wrong. I *attempted* to come out to my mother shortly after, and was only told that it's wrong to think like that. That I'm misguided and that people like that will go to hell.

I was nothing short of devastated. I had always been the good religious girl my family wanted me to be, and now my own religion rejects me? Why must I be sentenced to eternal damnation just for knowing who I am? Why does the whole world hate me for it?

Since then, whenever I ask my mother for anything, she always makes sure to tell me that "those people" are wrong and will go to hell if they don't repent. Like she knows something. I always smile and nod because I don't have the luxury of being my true self. I will never be able to move out and transition and live the life I want, it's just not what my future holds.

I've seen how we are treated by the Egyptian government. They think we're animals that don't deserve to live. They'll throw you in a cell and leave you to rot. There's not one day where I don't mourn Sarah Hegazi.

Needless to say, that severed the connection I had felt with my religion. Why should I have to follow a religion that wants me dead? Why did God create me just to tell me that I'm going to hell no matter what? Is it even worth it to do anything, knowing I'll never be who I want to be? That I'll actively be punished for it?

Why should I have to love the country that hates me? That persecutes people like me without batting an eye? That views me as a "national security threat"?

I still want to have hope for what the future holds. Who knows how long everything will keep being like this? I'd like to think everything will get better despite it all.

Journaling and This Website #

Posted October 5th, 2025

Earlier I was thinking about my website, and my journal, and I asked myself: Why did I even make a journal? I came to the conclusion that it was because I wanted to be seen, but I also realised that's the reason I made this whole website in the first place. Yeah, the first paragraph *does* say that I made this website because I got tired of linktrees and strawpages and whatever other websites are out there, but this is more the overall reason why I made this website.

Obviously I'm not going to write out my whole life story here, but to put it simply I had a few events during my childhood that ended up with me becoming quite the asocial (and paranoid) person. I still am, but I've been trying hard to remedy it.

I couldn't talk to people without thinking to myself, "What if they're friends with me as some kind of sick inside joke?" or, "What if, someday, I'm going to wake up and all of my friends will just be gone? What's the point of having friends, then?" I was afraid to even be *perceived* by people I didn't know. That's how bad it was!

I don't want to be stuck in a friendless limbo, nobody does. That's why I made this website in the first place, why I started talking to other people more. Hell, it's the reason why I even started to e-mail people and tell them how much I love their website, or their art, or really anything!

It's also why I'm here writing this post for whoever might read it... I just want to better myself.

AI #

Posted October 4th, 2025

I know it's been said many times by many different people, but I truly do hate how AI has been taking over the internet. Everywhere I go I see something about a "new AI feature" or I get a notification begging me to go try XYZ websites AI chatbot. Hell, even W3schools has an "AI helper" now.

Every new device has AI built-in nowadays. I can't even take a screenshot on my phone without being told to edit it using AI! I have to go out of my way to disable Copilot every time I open my laptop.

Remember Windows Recall? The thing that screenshots EVERYTHING on your desktop, even your passwords and personal info? You could tell me: Saif, we've been surveilled before the AI boom ever happened, don't be naive. Yes, but it still scares me. Privacy is like a fever dream now.

Every day I see more and more people use AI to think for them. It's even happening to my family! My classmates! I've seen my own mother use Gemini to look things up instead of just using a search engine. I've seen my classmates use ChatGPT to look up the simplest questions ever. And for what? So we can ruin the environment faster? So we can make ourselves dumber day by day? It's just so horrifying to watch.

I've used Firefox ever since I've known how to use a computer. Can you imagine how I felt when I saw "try our new AI chatbot" pop up after I updated it? I feel like I'm going crazy.

I could bring up a billion more reasons as to why I hate AI. AI being used for warfare, xAI taking water and clean air away from black families in Memphis, AI being used to bully artists and to steal and deface their work... Can you tell I don't like AI?

Autoplay #

Posted October 1st, 2025

There are many things I love about making and looking at websites. I love having something that is uniquely mine, and I love seeing websites where you can feel every bit of soul the author(s) put into them. I love the freedom that it gives you. You can basically do anything!

But there's one thing I absolutely cannot get myself to like no matter how hard I try. Autoplay. First of all, it's not accessible. Second of all, why are you trying to jumpscare me via audio file? I understand wanting people to listen to songs you like. I really do. But there HAS to be another way to do that, no?

Something that's even worse is when there's autoplay but no way to make it stop playing. I sometimes use a screen reader while I browse, and having an audio on autoplay and not being able to pause it is terrible. I imagine it must be terrible for others too.

And don't get me started on video autoplay. If I didn't know of the existence of pop-up blockers I probably would've lost my mind a long time ago. I could write an entire book about how much I hate video autoplay. (Also, who in their right mind thought it was a good idea to have video autoplay automatically enabled on YouTube?)

At the end of the day, you can do whatever you want with your website. I'm not going to hover over people and tell them not to put autoplay on their websites. Who am I to do that? But PLEASE at least put a warning that your website has autoplay on it... Thanks.
