Hello. I'm Saif. I use xe/xem/xeir/xeirs/xemself pronouns. I made this tacky website so I don't have to put my things on 50 different websites. I'm still new to HTML, so the website might look a little rough.

I am a lesbian that likes coffee, tea, drawing, and rocks. My favourite rock is Botswana Agate, and my favourite type of tea is English Breakfast.

My favourite characters are Pascal (NieR:Automata) and Kim Kitsuragi (Disco Elysium).

I speak both (Egyptian) Arabic and English. I have OCD and autism, both of which affect the way I act.

Feel free to send me a message anytime, I'll respond as quickly as I can. Please note that my way of talking may sound dry.
