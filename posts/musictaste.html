I wouldn't say I'm one for things like RYM, given how the community is, and because I struggle to assign a number to the music I listen to.

I saw this MusicTaste website, though, and I thought it was interesting! it's a small(er) community, which I like, and you can also contribute releases to the website! To me it feels like sharing the music I love with others.

I also find sites like these better at showing my music taste (haha get it) than Last.fm, because I kind of just loop the Song Of The Month that I like over and over until I get tired of it, which isn't great for showing off the music I like.
