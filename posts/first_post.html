So, for whatever reason I thought it would be a good idea to make a kind of journal. I seriously doubt anyone will read this, so that's a plus.

Recently, my iPhone 11 killed itself in classic apple device fashion, so I decided to get an Android because a. iPhones cost an arm and a leg and b. Who doesn't like sideloading support? (though that feature will be euthanized in 2027. Damn.) and I was met with a nice surprise: there's an android fork of Soulseek! This is huge for me because I use soulseek a lot to download my music and sometimes I'm just too lazy to get on my laptop to do things. I use Soulseek alongside Syncthing so I'm able to sync my music with my laptop and phone daily. Who needs Spotify?

Another recent discovery of mine is SomaFM . I've always beem semi-aware of internet radio but I never really thought about it. I decided to give it a try when i found out that foobar2000 had internet radio support and it blew my mind. I was introduced to so many great songs and it's like... how did I ever go this long without listening to this? I really hope that more people discover the greatness of SomaFM like I have.
